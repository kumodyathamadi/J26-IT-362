# Component 2 — Curated Legal Source Starter Pack

This folder is a curated selection from the user's uploaded `RP_PDF.zip`, grouped by likely relevance to the Sri Lankan SME business sectors in Component 2.

## How to use
1. Keep the original files unchanged; do not edit legal source PDFs.
2. Record metadata for each source: title, issuing authority, Act/Gazette number, date, language, source URL, date accessed, SHA-256 hash, and status (in-force / amended / repealed / draft / needs verification).
3. Extract text by page and preserve page numbers and section/clause references.
4. Convert only verified obligations into structured records with applicability conditions, evidence, authority, and source page/section.
5. Test each obligation against business profiles before using it for Mandatory / Conditional / Not Applicable classification.

## Important limitations
- This is a filename-based starter selection, not a legal review. Some numbered Gazette PDFs may be relevant but cannot be safely identified from their filenames alone.
- A document's presence does not prove that it is current, in force, or applicable to every business.
- The Companies Act relates to company incorporation; sole-proprietor/partnership business-name registration may need separate official guidance.
- Vehicle documents should be checked to confirm they apply to vehicle repair/garage businesses rather than only vehicle registration/import processes.
- Salon/cosmetics files may concern cosmetics product registration, not the operating licence for a salon itself.
- Draft regulations are not treated as binding law unless officially enacted and in force.

- Verify latest amendments, commencement dates, and current versions through official Sri Lankan authorities.
