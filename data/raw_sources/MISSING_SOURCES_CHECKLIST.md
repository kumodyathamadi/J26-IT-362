# Missing-source collection checklist

Use official government / authority websites where possible. Save PDFs and record the URL + access date.

- [ ] Business Names Ordinance + official sole-proprietor/partnership registration guidance and forms
- [ ] District Secretariat / Divisional Secretariat business-name registration procedure for target location
- [ ] Colombo Municipal Council trade licence requirements, by-laws and application forms (or the actual local authority for the selected address)
- [ ] Food Act regulations for food premises registration, hygiene, food handlers and relevant sector conditions
- [ ] Public health / local authority requirements for salons and barber shops
- [ ] Labour laws and official EPF / ETF employer registration guidance
- [ ] Current EPL prescribed-activity Gazette / environmental requirements relevant to the activity
- [ ] Official garage / vehicle repair / motor parts trading / resale licensing sources, as applicable
- [ ] Current official source for each Gazette/Act and its amendments or repeal status
