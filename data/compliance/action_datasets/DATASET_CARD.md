# Dataset Card

**Name:** Western Province SME Action Recommendation Dataset (360 records)

**Purpose:** Prototype and annotate next-action recommendations for SME business registration and compliance workflows in Western Province, Sri Lanka.

**Districts:** Colombo, Gampaha, Kalutara only.

**Categories:** business registration, food businesses, environmental/waste, vehicle repair/motor trade, data protection/digital, cosmetics/salon.

**Composition:** 120 records per district; 20 per district/category.

**Origin:** 240 filtered records from the supplied dataset (Colombo and Kalutara) plus 120 synthetically adapted Gampaha scenarios.

**Labels:** Draft action templates. All records require official-source verification and human review.

**Not suitable for:** Treating labels as verified legal truth, asserting local authority-specific requirements, or training a model to provide definitive legal advice without verification.

**Known limitation:** Gampaha rows provide scenario coverage only; they do not establish Gampaha-specific legal rules or procedures.
